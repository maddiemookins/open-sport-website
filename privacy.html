PROPOSED POLICY / NOT IN EFFECT

Privacy notice

We are preparing an appropriate UK privacy notice before opening public accounts or contact submissions.

Current public website

This information page does not collect login credentials or contact forms. Our interactive app demo uses fictional club information and may save fictional preferences locally on your device. Infrastructure providers may process technical request data, as described in their own applicable notices.

What a complete notice will explain

The identity and contact details of the data controller, the types of information collected, lawful bases, who receives information, international transfers, security, retention, access and erasure rights, complaints and age-appropriate safeguards.

Special protections

Our proposed design deliberately excludes sex assigned at birth from player registration. Children's accounts will be guardian-managed, with high privacy by default and appropriately limited communications. Sensitive dietary or emergency information will require additional restrictions.

Social sign-in and anti-abuse services

Google and Apple sign-in are not currently available through this website. If we introduce identity providers, bot prevention or analytics, the published policy will identify the providers, data sharing, cookies and settings, and any consent required.
