FOR EVERY TEAM

Less admin.
More playing.

From hockey and football to netball, cricket, rugby, basketball and volleyball, Open Sport is for all team sports.

Organise fixtures, select squads, track availability, manage match fees and match teas, share team news and celebrate the people who make your club special.

No Open Sport player subscriptions. Your club decides and collects its own fees.

THE LONG-TERM PLATFORM VISION

One platform.
Every level of sport.

Tools for a single team, a grassroots club, a regional league, a national governing body and international federations.

These capabilities form our product roadmap. The current demo covers only an early subset.

Members and clubs

Registration, teams, club administration, committees and insurance.

Competitions

Leagues, divisions, fixtures, results, tables and tournaments.

Match-day operations

Availability, squads, venues, officials, match reports and match teas.

Officials and coaching

Appointments, qualifications, training and development pathways.

Safeguarding and welfare

Child protections, training, welfare oversight and incident processes.

Governance and discipline

Member democracy, policies, complaints, disciplinary decisions and appeals.

Finance and facilities

Fees, grants, sponsorship, budgets, equipment and venue bookings.

Community and insight

Participation, recognition, volunteering, communications and reporting.

See the full list of sporting-body responsibilities in our draft charter.
