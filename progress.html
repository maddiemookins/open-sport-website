NEXT / THREE CONNECTED ROADMAPS

Making progress visible

These are working themes. Detailed milestones, owners, budgets and dates will be agreed as the Foundation develops. Items below are proposals, not delivery commitments.

01 / PRODUCT

Full Open Sport roadmap

Make the player and volunteer experience useful throughout the season.

  • Complete the evergreen guided demo.
  • Real club onboarding, fixtures, squads and messaging.
  • Find nearby clubs and plan away journeys.
  • Inclusive registration and team discovery.

Detailed roadmap: to be defined

02 / ENGINEERING

Technology roadmap

Reliable, low-maintenance infrastructure for sport in the real world.

  • Restore green CI and end-to-end acceptance.
  • Offline-first PWA and safe synchronisation.
  • Assess Android and iPhone app-store distribution.
  • OpenStreetMap integration, address quality and privacy.

Detailed roadmap: to be defined

03 / ORGANISATION

OSF roadmap

Establish a player-owned, volunteer-led sporting movement.

  • Founding charter and democratic governance.
  • Contact, feedback and volunteer onboarding.
  • Pilot clubs and local partnerships.
  • Funding, safeguarding and responsible growth.

Detailed roadmap: to be defined
