PRODUCT / PROPOSED SEQUENCE

Open Sport product roadmap

From a hockey demo to a platform for grassroots team sport. The stages show the proposed order. Delivery dates will be agreed as we learn from the pilot.

01 / Available to try

Hockey demo

Explore fixtures and the calendar, respond to availability and try Player of the Match voting with fictional data.

Try the demo →

02 / In review

Demo 1.0

Guided tour, offline demo loading, local profile photos and versioned release notes. The Foundation’s phone showcase is also in review.

Release candidate ↗

03 / Planned

Adult club pilot

Verify real sign-in and club setup. Run fixtures, availability, squads, teas, fee tracking, moderated chat and post-match voting with a pilot club.

Club onboarding checks ↗

04 / Core in review

Leagues and more sports

Build on the seven-sport competition core with verified results, scheduling, officials and published standings. Explore pick-up games and tournaments.

Competition core ↗

05 / Planned

Club community

Add supporter access, committee tools, AGM voting, insurance renewals and recognition for volunteers.

06 / Safeguarding review

Youth and schools

Plan guardian-managed under-16 access with nickname-only profiles, no profile photos and no direct messaging between children.

£0 Open Sport player subscription. Clubs set and collect their own fees. Our long-term goal is one million people playing amateur sport every week.
