01 / WHY WE EXIST

More playing.
More belonging.
More celebrating.

The best part of sport happens together. The team spirit, the friendships, the first match, the unexpected win and the joy of showing up.

Our mission is to make team sport accessible, affordable and welcoming for everyone. Participation, teamwork, friendships and belonging are central to Open Sport. We celebrate playing together, helping others and growing confidence.

INCLUSION / EVERYONE DESERVES TO BELONG

Everyone deserves to play.
Everyone deserves to belong.

Open Sport welcomes people of every gender, age, ability, identity, background and level of experience. We particularly want more women, girls and young people to discover the joy of team sport, including people who have never felt welcome at a club.

We prioritise participation, teamwork, friendship, health and belonging. Winning can be wonderful. Every person deserves to feel valued, supported and included, on and off the pitch.

Our success is measured by the people we bring together, the friendships we help create and the opportunities we open up.

ONE PLATFORM / EVERY LEVEL OF SPORT

From the dugout
to the federation.

Run your club from a dugout in the rain. Grassroots sport is powered by volunteers. We are building easy-to-use tools that work on your phone, wherever your team plays.

Our long-term ambition is a complete open-source platform to run a club, league, national governing body or international federation. The following capabilities describe our roadmap, not the full functionality of today's demo.

Membership and clubs

Registration, teams, volunteers, committees, membership and insurance.

Leagues and competitions

Fixtures, squads, results, tables, tournaments, promotion and relegation.

Officials and coaches

Umpire and referee appointments, coach qualifications and development pathways.

Safeguarding and welfare

Junior protections, safeguarding oversight, welfare processes and reporting.

Governance and integrity

Member voting, committees, constitutions, disciplinary cases and appeals.

Finances and facilities

Fees, sponsorship, grants, budgets, venues, equipment and bookings.

Community and recognition

Messaging, volunteering, player milestones, participation and achievements.

Insight and growth

Participation trends, inclusion measures and sporting-body reporting.

05 / FOUNDING CHARTER · DISCUSSION DRAFT

How we want
to work together.

Read our complete draft charter. The founding group will decide the legal structure, membership and voting rules, and how any national federations would work with the Foundation.

Belonging and shared responsibility

Everyone deserves dignity and a genuine chance to take part. We propose fair, evidence-based policies, clear ways to raise concerns, and safeguarding and privacy built in from the start.

A voice for the people who play

Players, clubs and volunteers should help make decisions. We propose elected leadership, published decisions and budgets, and independent routes to appeal.

Local clubs, shared tools

Clubs should retain local autonomy within shared standards. We propose open-source software and reinvesting any surplus in grassroots sport.
