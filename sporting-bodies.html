AUTOMATIC LEAGUE MANAGEMENT / PROPOSED

Rules you can understand.
Decisions you can explain.

01

Define the competition

Sport, season, division, points policy, tie-break order, promotion/relegation places and exceptional rules.

02

Record approved results

Apply wins, draws, losses, bonus points, forfeits and penalties according to that competition's published rules.

03

Calculate standings

Deterministic leaderboards show played, won, drawn, lost, points and tie-break evidence for every team.

04

Propose next season

Generate promotion/relegation and division-placement proposals, taking geography, facilities and travel into account.

Human oversight is essential

Competition organisers approve league structures, resolve appeals, verify eligibility and publish changes. Every automatic recommendation needs a traceable explanation and the ability to undo it. Travel optimisation and promotion are proposals until approved.
